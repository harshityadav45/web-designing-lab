ABES Engineering College (ABESEC), established in 2000 in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, is a private, AICTE-approved institution affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU). It holds an ‘A’ Grade accreditation from NAAC and is recognized for strong academic performance, consistently ranking among the top AKTU-affiliated colleges in the NCR region
